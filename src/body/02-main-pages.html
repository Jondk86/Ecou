Audio to sheet music

      
Choose an MP3 or WAV and Ecou writes out the notes, right on your phone. It works best with one instrument or a voice, recorded clearly.

      

      
Lead melody: the tune you would sing (top voice). Full song: lead, chords and bass on separate staves. Every note: one instrument such as a piano.

      
For a full song: Ecou splits it into vocals, bass, other instruments and drums, and writes a staff for each. The first time, it downloads the stem splitter (about 175 MB, kept on the phone). Splitting is slow on phones without GPU support, so you pick a section first.

Recording

      
      
      
      
Recordings are aligned automatically using the reported input and output latency. If takes still land early or late on this device, nudge the offset. With a multichannel interface, give each armed track its own input (In 1, In 2 or Stereo) and record them together. In a loop, every pass becomes a take.

      
Bluetooth headphones: while the mic is open, Android switches them to call mode (muffled, mono). Record with wired headphones or the phone speaker; Ecou releases the mic the moment recording stops.
